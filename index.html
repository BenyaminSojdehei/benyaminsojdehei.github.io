Research

Research &
publications.

Work on workforce effectiveness, simulation, and quality economics.

Published · ICISE 2026

Application of Overall Labor Effectiveness (OLE) and Root Cause Analysis for Workforce Performance Improvement

First-author paper presented in person at ICISE 2026. Found an annual average OLE of 81.44% against an ~85% benchmark, with quality as the primary limiting factor (94.08%) and two severe availability drops traced to misaligned incentives and inadequate risk-based planning.

In progress · Targeting IJPPM (Q1)

MHCPF: A Man-Hour and Cost-Based Productivity Framework

Converts operator productivity ratios into man-hour equivalents and monetary values, with quality-adjustment via rework deduction.
